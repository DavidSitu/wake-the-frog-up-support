What the app processes

DataPurpose
Account detailsEmail address, account identifier and display name support sign-in, account management and identifying you to your room. Supabase Auth handles password authentication.
Shared room activityRoom membership, room and companion names, room time zone, morning and rest plans, challenge type, care dates, completion records and shared history keep the group in sync.
Drawings and memoriesDrawing gestures, author identifiers, revisions and saved pixel snapshots provide the shared drawing and its history.
QR challengesA hash of the chosen QR target and challenge proofs are used to validate a QR task. Camera frames are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the app.
Safety and supportReports can include selected names or a drawing snapshot, a reason, related account and room identifiers, and timestamps. Blocks record the people you have blocked. Support emails contain information you choose to send.
Device and service stateSessions, pending actions, preferences, alarm scheduling state and temporary share files are stored on your device. Hosting and authentication providers may process network and operational logs to run and secure their services.

Who can see information

Members of your private room can see the names, shared companion, participation information, drawing and history that the app presents to the group. Your login email is not part of the room's normal display. Reports and blocking records are not displayed as a public feed.

The operator can access backend information to run the beta, investigate problems, handle reports and respond to account or privacy requests. Supabase provides authentication and database hosting; the beta database is located in Seoul, South Korea. Data may therefore be processed outside your country.

Apple provides TestFlight distribution and processes information under its own privacy terms. This support website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Email sent to the support address is processed by the email provider. These providers may keep their own operational records.

Camera, alarms and sharing

Camera access is used for QR scanning. Alarm permission is used for morning alarms you enable. You can manage these permissions in iOS settings. Choosing to share a memory creates a temporary image on the device and opens the system share sheet; the destination you choose then handles that copy.

The current app has no advertising or cross-app tracking integration. This support website adds no analytics scripts, forms or application cookies; the hosting service may process request logs.

Retention and deletion

Account and activity records are kept to provide the ongoing shared-room experience. You can request deletion through the app's account settings. The deletion flow removes your Auth identity and personal profile, plans, QR target, proofs, memberships and associated personal records. Names you authored are reset. Your drawing operations are removed and surviving drawing snapshots are rebuilt from remaining members' contributions.

Shared companions, generic room-history events and anonymous group outcomes can remain after account deletion. Leaving a room alone does not delete your account or all your past contributions. Copies that another person exported or shared are outside the app's deletion process.

Reports are marked for expiry after 30 days. During this beta, background purging is not yet enabled; records may remain until account deletion, report-related cleanup or operator cleanup. Contact the operator to request removal. An expiry timestamp alone does not guarantee that a record has been erased.

Provider logs, backup copies and support emails have separate retention from the live app database. We do not promise immediate removal from every provider record. Contact the operator to discuss what can be removed and any remaining retention.

Your choices

You can change your display name, leave a room, manage device permissions, use report/block controls, or delete your account. For access, correction or deletion requests that you cannot complete in the app, email the operator. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before handling a request; never send a password or verification code.
