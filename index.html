Support, feedback and account help for Wake the frog up.

Contact the developer

The app is operated by Heung Yu Mo. For a bug, account problem, privacy request or concern about shared content, contact:

ad.tesseract.studio@gmail.com

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the problem. Never send passwords, verification codes or access tokens.

Testing with your friends

  1. Use your own supplied test account. Do not share an account with another tester.
  2. Create a private room or join with a friend's invite code. A room supports up to four people.
  3. Check shared care, drawing updates and Memories together, including after closing and reopening the app.
  4. Use TestFlight feedback for screenshots and reproduction steps. Avoid including another person's private information.
Keep a separate trusted alarm. This is beta software. Native alarms require iOS 26 or newer and permission on your device. Stopping an alarm does not complete a wake-up challenge.

Login and password help

The first beta uses individually prepared test accounts. Self-service signup verification and password-reset emails are not available for ordinary tester addresses yet. Contact the developer if you cannot sign in. Do not send your password.

Shared content and safety

Keep names and drawings respectful. Do not share personal contact details, threats, harassment, hateful content or sexual content in a room. Share invite codes only with people you intend to invite.

Use the app's report and block controls when appropriate. A report acknowledges receipt; it does not automatically remove content or guarantee an immediate response. You can also email the developer with the room and approximate time of the concern. Avoid copying unnecessary personal information.

Delete your account

Use the account deletion action in the app's account settings. Deletion removes your login and personal app records, resets room or companion names you authored, and removes your drawing contributions. Shared companions and anonymous group outcomes can remain. If the deletion does not complete, contact the developer for help. Deleting a beta account means you will need a replacement account to test again.

Read the privacy information for the data involved and retention limits.
